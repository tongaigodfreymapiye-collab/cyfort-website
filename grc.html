CyFort Unified Control Crosswalk

A production GRC backend can maintain one control record and map it to multiple applicable requirements. Example: MFA evidence can contribute to an ISO control assessment, NIST CSF outcomes, applicable GDPR security obligations, DORA ICT controls and Zimbabwe data-security obligations—while MITRE ATT&CK mapping shows which adversary behaviours the technical control helps detect or mitigate.

Asset / Risk→CyFort Control→Evidence→Framework Mapping→Gap / Remediation
Framework support means assessment, mapping and advisory capability. It does not imply that CyFort is endorsed, accredited or certified by MITRE, NIST, ISO, the EU, PeopleCert or a regulator.
