Who we are

Cyber Fortress Technologies is a cybersecurity and technology risk consulting company helping organisations protect their digital assets, manage cyber risk, strengthen compliance, and build resilient technology environments.

Our approach combines cybersecurity expertise with business, technology, risk and supply-chain perspectives to help organisations understand exposure, strengthen controls, meet regulatory and industry requirements and respond effectively to an evolving threat landscape.

Our Mission

To make effective cybersecurity and risk management accessible, practical and aligned with business objectives. We don't just identify risks — we help organisations understand them, manage them and build stronger defences.

Our Vision

To become a trusted cybersecurity and technology risk partner for organisations in Zimbabwe, Africa and the global digital economy.

100% REMOTE. 100% CONNECTED

Cyber Fortress is a 100% remote cybersecurity company, delivering expert security, risk, compliance and advisory services without geographical boundaries.
